# Steering · Visual language

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| night | `#0A1016` | base background |
| deep | `#101922` | walls, floors |
| slate | `#16212A` | objects in shadow |
| cream | `#EDE6D8` | figures, paper, text |
| amber | `#E9A94B` | the single warm source |
| red | `#B5483B` | errors, missed calls, taillights |

## Hard rules

1. **No pure black.** The darkest value is `#050708`, and only for the final card.
   Night is deep blue-grey.
2. **One light per shot.** A lamp, a doorway, a phone screen, taillights. Everything
   else is falloff.
3. **One bright thing per frame.** If the viewer cannot say where to look, the frame
   is wrong.
4. **No outlines.** Shapes are solid fills; separation comes from value, not strokes.
5. **No gradients with more than three stops.** Banding on 8-bit is already a problem.

## Motion

- Cuts are hard. No dissolves except the single intentional one in shot 55.
- Camera moves are either a slow push or a lateral pan. Never a zoom-in-out.
- Anything that moves keeps moving for the whole shot. A shot that freezes reads as
  a still, and a still reads as a slide.

## Timing

- 4 seconds is the ceiling for a shot.
- A shot longer than 4s must contain at least three visual changes.
- Duration is set by the action, not by a budget: empty insert 1.5–2s, one complete
  action 2.5–3.5s.
