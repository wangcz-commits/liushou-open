# Steering · Product

## What this project is

A **code-drawn documentary short** about left-behind children in rural China.

Not a video edit, not stock footage, not generative video. Every frame is drawn by
Canvas 2D code, 30 times a second, deterministically — the same spec always produces
the same film.

## Why it exists

The subject matter is 15.5 million children whose parents work in cities. The usual
way to cover it is handheld footage or AI-generated "sad" clips. Both have a problem:
the viewer spends their attention deciding whether the image is real.

Flat vector removes that question. Nobody asks if a silhouette is a real child, so the
attention goes where it belongs — on what is happening.

## The one rule that matters

**Never show a face.** Characters are faceless silhouettes. Emotion is carried by
posture, occlusion, speed, and light. If a shot needs an expression to work, the shot
is wrong.

## What "done" means

A shot is done when a viewer can say what happened in it without reading the caption.
The caption explains; the picture states.
